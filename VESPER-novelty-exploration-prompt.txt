Microsoft's SkillOpt (github.com/microsoft/SkillOpt, arXiv 2605.23904) — a
text-space optimizer that treats one Markdown skill document as the trainable
state of a frozen LLM and improves it through rollout → reflect → bounded edit
→ held-out validation gate, plus a nightly consolidation mode (SkillOpt-Sleep).

That discovery materially changed the analysis. It was rated MEDIUM similarity:
its validation gate and its learning-rate / slow-update machinery genuinely
overlap our M3 and M4, while it has no counterpart at all for visual grounding,
per-step verification, or gap-driven skill creation.

That is the standard. Find the real, named, linked system that comes closest.
Admit overlap honestly where it exists. Then state precisely what survives.

A report that concludes "everything is novel" is a failed report.

HONESTY AND EVIDENCE RULES — FOLLOW STRICTLY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
· Every item you cite must have a real, working link. Never fabricate a paper
  title, an arXiv number, a patent number, or a repository name.
· If you cannot verify something, label it "unverified — needs manual check."
· Distinguish clearly between "I searched and found nothing" and "this does not
  exist." State which searches you ran and which terms you used.
· If a component's novelty looks weak, say so plainly and early. Overstating
  novelty is the most costly failure mode in this exercise.
· Where overlap exists, do not paper over it with wording differences. Identify
  which dimension the difference actually lives in:
      the TRIGGER (what starts it)
      the EVIDENCE / SIGNAL (what drives it)
      the UNIT OF CHANGE (what gets modified)
      the VALIDATION (how it is checked)
      the LATENCY (when it takes effect)
      the ENVIRONMENT (cloud vs on-device, simulated vs physical)
