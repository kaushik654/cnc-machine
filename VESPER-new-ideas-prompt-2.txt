VESPER — PROMPT 2 of 2:  NEW PATENTABLE IDEAS & EXTENSIONS

HOW TO USE
  Send this as a FOLLOW-UP message in the SAME ChatGPT conversation where you
  ran Prompt 1 (the novelty exploration on two components). Do not start a new
  chat — this prompt depends on the prior-art findings you just produced.

  If the conversation was lost, re-attach VESPER-Complete-Flow.docx and paste

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TASK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
You have just decomposed two components of VESPER and searched for existing
solutions. Now use those findings as raw material.

Generate NEW patentable ideas — improvements, extensions, and additions to these
two components and to the way they interact. These must be ideas that are NOT in
the attached disclosure and NOT in any prior art you found. This is invention
work, grounded in the gaps your own search just revealed.

Two rules govern everything below:

  RULE 1 — GROUND EVERY IDEA IN A FINDING.
  Each idea must trace back to something specific from your Prompt 1 analysis:
  a sub-module you marked NO SOLUTION FOUND, a limitation you noticed in the
  closest prior art, a weakness in our own mechanism, or an unexploited
  interaction between the two components. State that trace explicitly.

  RULE 2 — MECHANISM, NOT ASPIRATION.
  "Make retrieval smarter using context" is not an idea. "Maintain a per-screen
  failure fingerprint so that a repair attempt that failed on a visually
  equivalent screen is excluded from re-retrieval within the same session" is
  an idea. If you cannot state the data structure, the signal, the trigger and
  the decision rule, you have not produced an idea yet.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
GENERATE IDEAS IN FIVE CATEGORIES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Produce at least the stated number in each category. Quality over volume — but
do not skip a category.

  CATEGORY A — DEEPEN  (at least 3 ideas)
  Strengthen a sub-module that already exists in the disclosure by adding a
  mechanism that no prior art has. Aim at sub-modules you marked NO SOLUTION
  FOUND — that is where the white space is.

  CATEGORY B — EXTEND  (at least 3 ideas)
  Add a genuinely new capability to one of the two components — something the
  disclosure does not describe at all, but which fits its architecture and its
  on-device constraint.

  CATEGORY C — COUPLE  (at least 3 ideas)
  New interactions BETWEEN the two components under analysis, or between one of
  them and a third part of VESPER. Prioritise this category: prior experience on
  this invention has shown the coupling between loops is the strongest and least
  anticipated claim territory. Look for signals that one component produces and
  the other could consume but currently does not.

  CATEGORY D — RESCUE  (at least 2 ideas)
  For every sub-module you marked ANTICIPATED or PARTIALLY ANTICIPATED, propose
  a modification that restores novelty. Change the trigger, the signal, the unit
  of change, the validation, the latency, or the environment — whichever
  dimension the prior art does not occupy. Name the dimension you are moving.

  CATEGORY E — ADJACENT  (at least 2 ideas)
  Higher-risk, higher-reward directions: a capability that would matter on a
  real device, that borrows a mechanism from an adjacent field you encountered
  during the search (robotics, test automation, RPA, program synthesis,
  distributed systems, formal verification), and that nobody has applied to
  on-device skill agents.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
FORMAT FOR EACH IDEA — USE THIS TEMPLATE EXACTLY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  IDEA [A1 / B2 / C3 …] — <short memorable name>

  1. Traced from
     Which Prompt-1 finding produced this idea. Quote the row or verdict.

  2. Problem it solves
     Two sentences. What breaks today, for whom, and when.

  3. Mechanism
     The concrete design: data structures, signals, triggers, thresholds,
     decision rules, and the order in which they fire. This is the heart of the
     idea and should be the longest part. Be implementable.

  4. Worked example
     A short concrete trace on a real device scenario — use the Wi-Fi repair,
     the vague "fix this" storage request, the UI-drift case, or the
     Sunday-night skill-creation case from the disclosure.

  5. Where it plugs in
     Which component and sub-module it modifies or sits beside; what it consumes
     and what it emits; what changes in the end-to-end flow.

  6. Prior-art check  — MANDATORY, SEARCH AGAIN FOR THIS SPECIFIC IDEA
     Run fresh searches for this idea alone. Report:
       | Closest thing found | Owner | Year | Link | Why this idea still differs |
     If you find the idea already exists, say so and either drop the idea or
     state what modification would restore novelty. Do not skip this step —
     an unchecked idea is worthless for a filing.

  7. Novelty statement
     One sentence: "X, characterised in that Y, wherein Z."

  8. Claim sketch
     One independent claim in plain structured language, plus two dependent
     claims. Not final legal drafting — enough for an attorney to work from.

  9. On-device feasibility
     Compute, memory, latency and battery implications. Does it run inside a
     phone's budget? What is the cheapest viable version? If it cannot run
     on-device, say so — that is disqualifying for this invention.

  10. Risks and failure modes
      What breaks it, what makes it dangerous, what a patent examiner will
      attack first.

  11. Scores  (1-5 each, with a one-line justification per score)
      Patentability   — how clear is the white space
      Feasibility     — how buildable on-device, near-term
      Impact          — how much better does the product actually get
      Claim strength  — how hard is it to design around

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
AFTER THE IDEAS — THREE CLOSING SECTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  CLOSING 1 — RANKED PORTFOLIO TABLE
  Every idea in one table, sorted by total score:
  | ID | Name | Category | Patentability | Feasibility | Impact | Claim strength |
  | Total | One-line recommendation: FILE NOW / DEVELOP FURTHER / PARK / DROP |

  CLOSING 2 — THE TOP THREE, DEFENDED
  For the three highest-ranked ideas, write a paragraph each arguing why this
  should be added to the disclosure, and a paragraph anticipating the strongest
  objection a reviewer or examiner would raise — and how it is answered.

  CLOSING 3 — WHAT NOT TO PURSUE
  Ideas you generated internally and rejected, with the reason. This is as
  valuable as the accepted list: it stops the team re-proposing dead ends.
  Include anything you found to be already solved by existing systems.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
CONSTRAINTS — ALL IDEAS MUST RESPECT THESE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  · ON-DEVICE. Everything must run locally on a phone. No cloud calls, no
    server-side training, no external API dependency. An idea requiring a
    stronger teacher model in the cloud is out of scope.

  · DO NOT TOUCH THE UNCLAIMED ZONE. ASR / speech input, execution-context
    building (310), planning and orchestration mechanics (330), and action
    synthesis and backend routing (410 / 420) are deliberately not claimed.
    Do not propose novelty there.

  · RESPECT THE EXISTING ARCHITECTURE. Ideas should fit the two-loop design
    (V1-V4 runtime, M1-M4 creation) over the shared knowledge layer (220
    embedding space, 230 step index, 240 versioned skill graph, 250 tripartite
    memory). An idea that requires discarding that architecture is a different
    invention — note it separately rather than mixing it in.

  · SAFETY AND GOVERNANCE MATTER. This agent creates its own skills and acts on
    a personal device. Any idea that increases autonomy must say how it stays
    reviewable, bounded, and reversible.

  · NO FABRICATION. Every link, paper, patent number and repository you cite
    must be real and verifiable. Label anything unverified as such.

  · BE HONEST ABOUT WEAK IDEAS. If a category yields only mediocre material,
    say so rather than padding. A short list of strong ideas beats a long list
    of plausible-sounding ones, and padding wastes attorney time.
